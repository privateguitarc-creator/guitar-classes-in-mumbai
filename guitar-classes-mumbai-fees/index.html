How much do guitar classes cost in Mumbai?

For Guitar Classes in Mumbai, the current published price starts at ₹999 for a 30-minute private home lesson. A 1-hour pay-as-you-go lesson is ₹1,949. The complete 24-session course is ₹44,999, which works out to about ₹1,875 per 1-hour session when all 24 sessions are completed.

These are private doorstep lessons: the instructor travels to the learner's home. They should not be compared directly with large group classes, online-only lessons or academy batches because the lesson format is different.

How group pricing works

The current published pricing is for the lesson rather than a separate fee for each person. If two or more people learn together at the same location, the website states that the same session fee applies to the group. This can materially reduce the effective cost per learner.

Important: Prices on this page reproduce the business's currently published rates. Availability, travel feasibility and commercial terms can change, so confirm the final amount before making a payment.

Fee questions

Is travel charged separately?

The current published pricing describes coaching and convenience as included. Confirm your location before booking because Mumbai coverage depends on practical travel availability.

Is there a free class?

The website currently lists the first class as free only with the 24-session complete-course option, not with pay-as-you-go sessions.

Can two people share one fee?

The current published model states that people learning together at the same location can share the same lesson fee.

How much is a 1-hour guitar class in Mumbai with this service?

The current pay-as-you-go price is ₹1,949 for one hour. Under the 24-session course, the effective price is about ₹1,875 per one-hour session when all 24 sessions are completed.

Can I learn guitar in 30 days?

Thirty days can be enough to begin learning basic chords, rhythm and simple song sections, but progress depends on practice, coordination and prior musical experience. This service does not promise a fixed result within 30 days.

The service covers multiple Mumbai areas through home tuition. Availability depends on the exact location, preferred time and the instructor's travel schedule. See the Mumbai areas served page or send your area before booking.
