What the 24-session guitar course is designed to develop

The 24-session option is a complete-course format rather than 24 identical lessons. The exact order can change with the learner, but the teaching focus is built around practical guitar skills that become more useful together over time.

Clean chords and smoother changes

Build reliable fretting, reduce muted notes and make common chord transitions more comfortable.

Rhythm and strumming

Develop steadier timing and usable strumming patterns so songs feel musical rather than mechanical.

Playing while singing

When this matches the learner's goals, guitar rhythm and vocal timing can be developed together gradually.

Listening and independence

Learn to notice chord movement, rhythm and song structure so you become less dependent on step-by-step tutorials over time.

No fixed-result promise: progress differs by learner, practice, coordination and previous musical experience. The 24 sessions define the course format, not a guaranteed skill level by a fixed date.

How group pricing works

The fee is for the session. If two or more people learn together at the same home, they share that fee. For example, two learners sharing a one-hour lesson at ₹1,949 would each contribute ₹974.50.

Important: Share your locality and the number of people learning together when enquiring. Confirm the lesson length, travel availability and final amount before paying.
