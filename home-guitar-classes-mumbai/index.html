Home Guitar Classes in Mumbai

Learn in your own home instead of travelling to a music academy. The program is built around personal instruction, flexible scheduling and a learning plan that adapts to your goals.

Ask about availability

How doorstep guitar lessons work

Guitar Classes in Mumbai is a home-tuition service founded in 2017 by Aniket Gawande. Lessons take place at the learner's home, so the student does not need to commute to a fixed classroom. Sessions can be booked for an individual learner or for people learning together at the same location.

Personal instruction

The lesson can focus on your current level, song goals, rhythm, chord changes, technique and musical understanding instead of following the pace of a large batch.

No student commute

The instructor travels to the learner. This is especially useful when work, school or Mumbai travel time makes a fixed academy schedule difficult.

Flexible session lengths

The current program offers 30-minute, 1-hour, 1.5-hour and 2-hour pay-as-you-go formats, plus a 24-session course.

Learn together

The current fee model also allows multiple people at the same location to learn together under one lesson fee rather than charging each participant separately.

What the lessons are designed to develop

The emphasis is not only on copying songs. The program focuses on practical guitar independence: rhythm, chord changes, musical listening, playing while singing, and understanding enough of the instrument to work out music more confidently on your own.

Who this format can suit

Home lessons can suit first-time learners, adults with demanding schedules, families who prefer learning at home, and two or more people who want to share a session. Current learners on the main program range from age 12 to 65.

Before booking: Mumbai is a large service area. Confirm your address and preferred timing directly so travel availability can be checked before a session is scheduled.

Home lessons versus travelling to an academy

An academy can be useful for students who specifically want a classroom, fixed batch and peer environment. Home tuition is different: its value is personal attention, no learner commute and the ability to learn in the same environment where you will normally practise.

Common questions

Are the classes only one-to-one?

No. The service supports individual lessons and people learning together at one location.

Do you teach complete beginners?

Yes. The main program is designed to start from the learner's current level, including complete beginners.

Do I need to travel?

No for the doorstep format—the instructor comes to the learner's home, subject to service-area and schedule availability.
