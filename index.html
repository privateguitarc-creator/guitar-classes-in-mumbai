1. I'm a beginner. Can I really learn to play?

Answer: The linked example shows a first-time learner beginning to combine simple guitar playing and singing in her first lesson.

2. I don't know how to sing. I'm very bad at it.

Answer: The linked example shows a learner combining guitar and singing by her third class. Progress varies by learner, practice and prior musical familiarity.

▶

3. Why should I join when I can learn elsewhere?

Answer: This program emphasizes practical music sense alongside chords, rhythm and listening, with the goal of helping learners become more independent rather than relying only on tabs or tutorials.

▶

4. Why pay for a hobby when YouTube is free?

Answer: YouTube can be useful for self-learning. Private lessons add live correction, structure and accountability. The linked example shows one learner playing and singing during his fourth class.

▶

5. I'm an adult with a busy routine. Is it too late for me?

Answer: Adults can learn too. Home tuition removes the learner’s commute and can make it easier to fit lessons around work and family schedules.

▶

6. Isn't playing and singing together super difficult?

Answer: When it matches the learner’s goals, lessons can introduce rhythm and vocal timing together early so playing and singing develop as a coordinated skill.

▶

7. Do I need complex theory or high-end gear first?

Answer: Absolutely not. We bypass boring paper theory and focus on practical ear training and chord flow on any basic guitar so you play real music right away.
