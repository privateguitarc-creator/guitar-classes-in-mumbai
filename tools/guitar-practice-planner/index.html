What should a beginner practise?

A useful beginner session usually mixes a short warm-up, chord changes, rhythm or strumming, and time spent applying those skills to a song. The exact balance depends on what you are currently struggling with, which is why every block above can be edited.

Keep it measurable: write down one thing you want to improve before starting. Examples: cleaner G–C switches, steadier eighth-note strumming, or playing one verse without stopping.
